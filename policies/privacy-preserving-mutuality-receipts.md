# Privacy-Preserving Mutuality Receipts

## Purpose

Privacy-Preserving Mutuality Receipts provide evidence of a narrow, current,
platform-scoped state, such as reciprocal interest or an active consent grant.

They do not measure character, safety, popularity, trustworthiness,
compatibility, desirability, relationship value, or reputation.

## Permitted receipt predicates

A receipt may prove only one of the following:

- The holder satisfies Hearthline's current 30+ adult-membership policy.
- A reciprocal-interest window is currently active for a named interaction scope.
- A specific capability consent grant is currently active for a specific recipient
  scope and expiry.
- A public-first meeting plan has mutual acceptance.
- A named-venue disclosure envelope is active and unexpired.
- A receipt was revoked, expired, or superseded.

## Prohibited receipt predicates

A receipt must never represent:

- Safety.
- Trust.
- Reputation.
- Popularity.
- Desirability.
- Respectability.
- Sexual experience.
- Number of encounters.
- Number of matches.
- Number of reports or blocks.
- Absence of reports or blocks.
- Compliance personality.
- Consent quality.
- Medical status.
- Sobriety.
- Identity authenticity.
- Financial status.
- Housing status.
- Employment status.
- Social-network standing.
- “Good actor” or “good member” status.

## Non-transferability

Receipts are:

- Scope-bound.
- Purpose-bound.
- Time-limited.
- Revocable.
- Non-transferable.
- Non-accumulative.
- Not publicly searchable.
- Not visible in discovery.
- Not usable for ranking, matching, moderation, advertising, or model training.

## Credential privacy

Where cryptographic techniques are used:

- Disclose the minimum predicate necessary.
- Avoid persistent holder identifiers.
- Prevent cross-context correlation where feasible.
- Do not expose issuer history, credential metadata, or interaction history.
- Do not require DID publication, public-key publication, blockchain publication,
  wallet creation, or public credential storage.
- Provide a fully functional non-cryptographic equivalent path.

## Member-facing language

> This receipt only confirms a limited current Hearthline state. It does not
> evaluate either person or promise safety, trust, consent to anything else, or a
> particular outcome.
