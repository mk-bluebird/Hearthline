# Access Preference and Interaction Options Router

## Purpose

The Access Preference and Interaction Options Router helps members find and plan
connection options that align with their selected interaction, communication,
sensory, mobility, scheduling, and affordability preferences.

It does not diagnose, label, infer, verify, or disclose disability, health,
neurodivergence, age-related condition, recovery, competence, or identity.

## Member-selected controls

A member may select and revise practical controls such as:

- Text first.
- Captions requested.
- Keyboard-only interaction.
- Screen-reader-compatible path.
- Reduced-motion path.
- No camera.
- No microphone.
- No voice requirement.
- Asynchronous messages.
- Short-message preference.
- Quiet setting preferred.
- Lower-sensory setting preferred.
- Seated option preferred.
- Step-free venue preferred.
- Public-transit information preferred.
- Independent arrival preferred.
- Remote-first option.
- Flexible timing.
- Low-cost activity preference.

Each control is optional, private by default, editable, and removable.

## Venue and activity data rules

The router may use only source-labeled public information such as:

- Venue-published step-free entrance information.
- Venue-published seating information.
- Venue-published captioning or interpretation information.
- Venue-published noise, lighting, or sensory-event guidance where available.
- Venue-published transit, parking, and public-access information.
- Event organizer's published schedule.
- Public cost information when published.

The router must show:

- Source/publisher.
- Source URL.
- Retrieval date.
- Review date.
- Whether the information is unknown or unverified.
- A plain-language statement that venue conditions can change.

The router must not claim that a venue is currently open, safe, accessible,
appropriate, quiet, affordable, free of barriers, or suitable for a particular
person unless a current source explicitly states a limited factual attribute.

## Disclosure rules

- Access preferences remain private by default.
- A member may share one selected preference with one selected counterpart for
  a stated purpose, such as planning a public meeting.
- A recipient may not see which preferences a member omitted, withdrew, or
  declined to share.
- The platform must not disclose a member's diagnosis, identity, or reason for
  any preference.
- A member may use every core connection feature without sharing any access
  preference.

## Ordering rules

- There is no global "accessibility-first" sort that ranks people.
- The member may choose to prioritize options matching their own currently
  selected controls.
- The system must not rank venues by presumed quality or accessibility.
- The system may filter or group results based on source-labeled attributes
  selected by the member.
- Unknown venue attributes must remain unknown, not treated as negative evidence
  about a venue or a member.

## Non-discrimination rules

Access and comfort controls must not be used for:

- Matching or excluding people.
- Discovery ranking.
- Visibility allocation.
- Advertising.
- Pricing.
- Risk or trust scoring.
- Eligibility decisions.
- Health or disability inference.
- Moderator profiling.
- Research cohorts defined by presumed disability or identity.
