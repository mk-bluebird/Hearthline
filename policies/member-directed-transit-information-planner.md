# Member-Directed Transit Information Planner

## Purpose

The Member-Directed Transit Information Planner helps adults independently review
public transit and venue information while planning an optional public-first
meeting.

It does not track location, calculate a person's route, determine safety,
guarantee transit service, monitor attendance, infer routine, arrange transport,
or require travel.

## Permitted planning inputs

- Member-selected broad meeting area.
- Member-selected venue category.
- Mutually approved named public venue.
- Broad future time window.
- Independent-arrival preference.
- Independent-departure preference.
- Publicly published transit-agency information.
- Publicly published venue hours and accessibility information.
- Remote-first or text-first alternative.

## Prohibited inputs

- Home address.
- Work address.
- Current location.
- GPS coordinates.
- Route origin or destination.
- Real-time transit position.
- Boarding or arrival status.
- Travel history.
- Routine schedule.
- Exact availability inferred from account behavior.
- Counterpart location.
- Counterpart transit history.
- Device proximity.
- Location sharing as a condition of using the planner.

## Required member-facing statement

> Transit and venue information comes from public sources and can change.
> Hearthline does not track your trip, guarantee a route or service, or determine
> whether a route, venue, or meeting is safe. You can choose a remote option,
> a different time, or no meeting.

## Independent travel rule

Hearthline must not facilitate transportation conditioned on intimacy, affection,
continued contact, money, housing, employment, debt relief, or another material
exchange.

A member may state:

- “I prefer to arrange my own travel.”
- “I am not discussing transportation.”
- “Public-first only.”
- “Remote-first works better for me.”

No explanation is required.

## Source rules

Every transit or venue information entry must include:

- Publisher/source name.
- Source URL.
- Retrieval time.
- Review-by time.
- Plain-language summary.
- Status: listed, under_review, outdated, or removed.

The planner must clearly label unknown or outdated information.
