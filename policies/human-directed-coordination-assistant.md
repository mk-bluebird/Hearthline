# Human-Directed Coordination Assistant

## Purpose

The Human-Directed Coordination Assistant helps a member privately prepare an
editable invitation or question using only options the member has actively
selected for that draft.

It does not negotiate, communicate, consent, schedule, or act on behalf of a
member.

## Permitted assistance

The assistant may:

- Draft a public-first activity invitation.
- Draft a remote-first alternative.
- Draft a question about pace.
- Draft a question about whether another adult wants to continue conversation.
- Restate the member's selected text-first or public-first preference.
- Offer neutral decline language.
- Show a checklist of later consent checkpoints.

## Prohibited assistance

The assistant must not:

- Send messages, reactions, likes, invitations, reports, or external actions.
- Negotiate with another member, bot, service, or agent.
- Express or receive consent.
- Create, accept, decline, alter, or interpret a consent grant.
- Reveal or infer exact availability, routine, location, route, venue attendance,
  identity, health, recovery, finances, housing, employment, or vulnerability.
- Read calendars, messages, device activity, transit data, location data, or
  social graph data to create a proposal.
- Propose private venues, transport, lodging, money, gifts, employment, debt
  relief, drugs, or any material exchange.
- Optimize toward reply, meeting, sexual activity, engagement, conversion, or
  relationship outcome.
- Make a claim about another person's preferences, capacity, intent, or consent.

## Required member confirmation

Before a draft can leave the member's device or be placed in a message composer,
the interface must show:

1. The exact draft text.
2. Every member-selected input used.
3. The intended recipient, if any.
4. A statement that the draft is not consent and creates no obligation.
5. A clear choice to edit, discard, or manually send through the ordinary
   messaging flow.

The assistant must not have a send capability.
