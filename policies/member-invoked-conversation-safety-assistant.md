# Member-Invoked Conversation Safety Assistant Policy

## Purpose

The Member-Invoked Conversation Safety Assistant helps a member draft, review,
understand, or report selected conversation content.

It is optional, member-initiated, advisory, and limited to the content the
member actively submits for that task.

## Permitted tasks

- Draft a boundary.
- Draft a respectful decline.
- Draft a public-first invitation.
- Draft a request for slower pacing.
- Explain a consent checkpoint.
- Summarize selected current preferences already shared in the interaction.
- Identify plain-language pressure patterns in member-selected text.
- Suggest non-accusatory questions.
- Prepare a member-reviewed report draft from selected text.
- Offer block, mute, restrict, report, and human-review options.

## Prohibited tasks

The assistant must not:

- Monitor all messages by default.
- Infer identity, sexuality, disability, health, recovery, sobriety, emotion,
  trauma, diagnosis, vulnerability, finances, housing, employment, or intent.
- Assign safety, risk, trust, truthfulness, manipulation, compatibility,
  desirability, or moral scores to a person.
- Determine whether an offline act is legal, consensual, safe, or criminal.
- Automatically send a message, report, block, restrict, notify, suspend, or
  contact an external party.
- Pressure a member to disclose, forgive, continue contact, meet, provide media,
  share location, or use a particular safety workflow.
- Retain selected chat text beyond the task's documented retention period.
- Use selected chat text for discovery, matching, advertising, analytics
  profiling, or default model training.

## Output rules

Outputs must:

- Clearly say they are advisory.
- Identify uncertainty.
- Avoid diagnostic or accusatory language.
- Encourage member choice.
- Offer a quiet-exit route.
- Distinguish a platform policy concern from legal advice.
- Avoid repeating intimate text unnecessarily.
- Allow the member to edit or discard all drafts.

## Human review and appeal

- An account-level restriction cannot be imposed solely because of assistant output.
- Reports are submitted only by the reporting member.
- Material moderation decisions require documented policy and human review.
- Affected members must have an accessible correction and appeal route.
- Review records must be content-minimized and retention-limited.
