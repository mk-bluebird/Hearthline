# Current Connection Intent Card

## Purpose

The Current Connection Intent Card lets adults state what they are open to now:
friendship, conversation, shared activities, dates, romance, intimacy, casual
connection, or an undecided path.

Intent is descriptive. It is not consent, a promise, a contract, a prediction,
a demand for response, or permission to escalate.

## Core rules

1. A member may select multiple intents, revise them, pause them, or remove them
   at any time.
2. A member may select no intent and still use core account, privacy, reporting,
   accessibility, and resource features.
3. A shared intent may support a discovery option only when both members
   independently selected compatible current intent and active discovery consent.
4. A shared intent does not open messaging, sexual discussion, media sharing,
   voice/video, venue disclosure, meeting planning, recording, or AR access.
5. Each later capability requires its own consent checkpoint.
6. Intent may not be used to calculate sexual availability, vulnerability,
   desirability, popularity, compatibility, response likelihood, or risk.
7. Intent may not be used to target advertising or create a public search index.
8. Casual connection and intimacy must never be connected to money, housing,
   employment, debt relief, transportation dependency, drugs, gifts conditioned
   on intimacy, or basic needs.

## Pacing controls

Members may separately select non-diagnostic current preferences such as:

- Text first.
- Public first.
- Conversation first.
- Low-pressure.
- Gentle pacing.
- Short activity preferred.
- Slower replies welcome.
- No photo required.
- Ask before sexual topics.
- Not discussing personal context yet.
- No answer needed.

These controls must not be interpreted as evidence of health, recovery, crisis,
disability, emotional state, capacity, or vulnerability.

## Prohibited categories

The Intent Card must not ask members to select:

- Stress relief.
- Loneliness.
- Crisis.
- Trauma.
- Emotional instability.
- Financial need.
- Housing need.
- Transportation dependence.
- Recovery status.
- Health status.
- Disability label.
- Sexual availability score.
- Sexual role requirement.
- Risk classification.
- Safety classification.
- Willingness to obey.
- Desire to be rescued.
- Dependence on another person.

## Required member-facing reminder

> What you choose here describes what you are open to right now. It is not
> consent to a message, photo, call, meeting, location sharing, sexual topic,
> recording, touch, or intimacy. You can change it at any time.
