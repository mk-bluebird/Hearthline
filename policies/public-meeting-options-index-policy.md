# Public Meeting Options Index Policy

## Purpose

The Public Meeting Options Index helps members independently review source-labeled
public information about possible public meeting settings.

It does not certify safety, guarantee accessibility, track attendance, recommend
a venue for a particular person, rate venues, establish trustworthiness, or
determine whether a meeting should occur.

## Permitted venue information

The Index may store only source-labeled public information, including:

- Venue category.
- Official venue or publisher name.
- Official source URL.
- Publicly published hours or event schedule.
- Publicly published transit information.
- Publicly published step-free entrance information.
- Publicly published seating information.
- Publicly published captioning or interpretation information.
- Publicly published quiet-period or low-sensory information.
- Publicly published parking information.
- Publicly published cost information.
- Publicly published contact information.
- Retrieval date.
- Review-by date.
- Source status.

## Required member-facing statement

> This information comes from public sources and may change. Hearthline does not
> certify that any venue is safe, open, accessible, affordable, appropriate, or
> suitable for you or for a particular meeting.

## Prohibited venue information

The Index must not store, infer, display, rank, or use:

- “Safe,” “verified safe,” “trusted,” “low risk,” or equivalent labels.
- Crime or policing predictions.
- Community safety votes, ratings, or crowd reports.
- Identity-group suitability claims.
- Dating, casual-encounter, or intimacy suitability claims.
- Member attendance, check-ins, real-time presence, or past visits.
- Venue popularity among members.
- Meeting outcomes, report counts, blocks, disputes, or relationship outcomes.
- Home/work proximity, distance from a member, routes, or travel history.
- Location histories, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth observations, or device proximity.
- Venue-level trust, safety, desirability, or risk scores.
- Any person-to-venue edge or counterpart-to-venue edge.

## Community reporting boundary

Members may report inaccurate public-source information, broken links, stale
details, accessibility-information errors, or abuse occurring inside Hearthline's
meeting-planning tools.

Members must not be asked to rate whether a venue is safe, whether someone they
met was safe, whether an identity group belongs at a venue, or whether another
member attended a venue.

Reports about real-world criminal conduct, emergencies, or immediate danger must
not be treated as venue-ranking inputs. The interface may show general emergency
guidance and encourage use of appropriate local emergency or support channels
without tracking the member's location.

## Ordering rules

Members may filter or group Index entries by their own selected practical
preferences and source-labeled attributes.

The Index must not sort venues by:

- Safety.
- Proximity to a member.
- Popularity.
- Other members' activity.
- Identity-group relevance.
- Report volume.
- Engagement.
- Likelihood of a date, casual encounter, or intimacy.
