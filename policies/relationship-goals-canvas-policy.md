# Relationship Goals Canvas Policy

## Purpose

The Relationship Goals Canvas lets members describe what forms of connection,
pace, planning, and future discussion they are open to now.

It is an editable current-context tool. It is not consent, a relationship
contract, a promise of commitment, a requirement to respond, or permission for
later escalation.

## Renegotiation rules

- A member may revise, narrow, broaden, pause, or withdraw any dimension at any time.
- A revision takes effect immediately for future discovery and interaction flows.
- A prior canvas does not remain usable after withdrawal or expiry.
- A changed goal must not trigger a penalty, explanation demand, visibility
  reduction, or reputation effect.
- A recipient may see that a currently shared goal is different only if it
  remains within the member's chosen disclosure scope.
- The system must not reveal prior values to new recipients.
- An intent change does not alter an existing consent grant unless the member
  separately withdraws that capability.

## Mutual-context presentation

The system may display:

- Shared current selections.
- Different current selections.
- Categories one or both members are not discussing.

The system must not display:

- Goal scores.
- Commitment probability.
- Long-term potential.
- Likelihood of intimacy.
- Relationship success prediction.
- “Better” or “worse” relationship goals.
- One person's private omitted or withdrawn values.

## Required reminder

> These are current goals and discussion preferences. They are not a promise,
> consent, exclusivity agreement, or permission to escalate. Either person can
> revise or stop at any time.
