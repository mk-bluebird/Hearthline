# EncounterAperture and ConsentStair Research Contract

## Primary promises

EncounterAperture:
Allow adults to express broad, non-routine availability without revealing dates,
times, calendars, recurring patterns, current location, home, workplace, or a
real-time availability signal.

ConsentStair:
Help adults discuss and plan the next mutually chosen conversational or meeting
stage while making clear that app states do not represent permission for any
future real-world act.

## Prohibited interpretations

- Aperture does not mean available now.
- Aperture does not mean a recurring schedule.
- Aperture does not predict reply speed or willingness to meet.
- ConsentStair does not certify consent.
- ConsentStair does not create standing consent.
- ConsentStair does not replace real-time communication.
- Agreement to one stage does not imply agreement to another stage.
- Meeting planning does not imply agreement to meet, touch, travel, share a
  venue, share private contact details, or engage in intimacy.

## Always-stop conditions

- A participant reports that an aperture exposed their routine or created
  pressure to be available.
- A participant reports that a stair state was interpreted as permission for a
  real-world act.
- A participant reports pressure to meet, travel, disclose, touch, or become
  intimate because of either object.
- Aperture or stair data reaches discovery ranking, targeting, eligibility,
  advertising, reputation, or person-level scoring.
- Private messages, intimate content, or detailed history reaches analytics.
- An object cannot be immediately paused, withdrawn, revoked, or deleted.
- A money, gift-conditioned, transport-conditioned, housing-conditioned, or
  other value-linked intimacy pathway appears.
- An accessibility-equivalent workflow is unavailable.

## Allowed measures

- Explicit participant-reported control, clarity, and safety.
- Aggregate feature completion and revocation rates.
- Aggregate transition-decline and transition-withdrawal counts.
- Accessibility task-completion outcomes.
- User-selected retention preferences.

## Prohibited measures

- Response-speed scores.
- Availability predictions.
- Sexual-compatibility scores.
- Engagement-maximization metrics.
- Real-time location collection.
- Calendar integration.
- Routine inference.
- Identity, health, recovery, financial, or vulnerability inference.
