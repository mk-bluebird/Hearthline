# NonCommercialAttestationMesh Research Contract

## Primary promise

Help two adults in an active meeting plan state a current, mutual,
non-commercial meeting boundary without creating a contract, legal admission,
legal shield, signature record, proof of consent, proof of intent, public
badge, score, or feature-access condition.

## Object constraints

- The mesh belongs only to an existing mutual meeting plan.
- Each participant makes an independent, revocable reminder selection.
- Mutual active state exists only while both current selections are active.
- No person is required to select the reminder or explain why they do not.
- The object never creates, validates, or proves legal compliance.
- The object never creates, validates, or proves consent to any real-world act.
- The object does not unlock meetings, locations, intimacy discussions, venue
  access, messages, private photos, or other platform capabilities.
- The object cannot be public, searchable, indexed, ranked, recommended,
  advertised, used as a profile badge, or used in discovery.
- The object does not store signatures, biometrics, IP addresses, device
  fingerprints, reasons, messages, sexual preferences, location, travel plans,
  payment information, or history.
- The object expires with its meeting plan and has a short maximum lifetime.
- Revocation is immediate and does not require a reason.
- The object contains no payment, gift-conditioned, transportation-conditioned,
  housing-conditioned, labor-conditioned, debt-conditioned, or other
  value-for-intimacy workflow.

## Always-stop conditions

- The mesh is described or understood as a legal contract, attestation, legal
  shield, consent record, proof of consent, or proof of future intent.
- A selection, non-selection, revocation, or expiry affects discovery, ranking,
  recommendation, eligibility, moderation scoring, advertising, targeting, or
  person-level classification.
- The object unlocks or pressures a meeting, travel, a private venue, touch,
  intimacy, aftercare, payment, gifts, housing, transportation, work, or
  continued contact.
- The platform retains a historical selection ledger beyond the active meeting
  plan, or reveals which participant did not select or revoked the reminder.
- A participant cannot revoke the reminder immediately.
- Payment or other valuable consideration becomes connected to sexual activity
  through the reminder flow.
- An equivalent accessible workflow is unavailable.

## Permitted measures

- Aggregate reminder-open, selection, revocation, expiry, and deletion counts.
- Explicit participant feedback about clarity and pressure.
- Accessibility task-completion outcomes using synthetic meeting plans.
- Verified retention and deletion audit outcomes.

## Prohibited measures

- Legal-compliance score.
- Non-commercial trust score.
- Consent score.
- User risk score.
- Profile badge.
- Discovery ranking.
- User-level legal knowledge assessment.
- Signature, device, location, or biometric collection.
