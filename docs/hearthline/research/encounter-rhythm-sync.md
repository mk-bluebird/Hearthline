# EncounterRhythmSync Research Contract

## Primary promise

Enable two adults in an active mutual corridor to discuss current pacing,
planning, conversation, affection-discussion, and follow-up preferences without
creating a compatibility score, a discovery signal, a permanent sexual profile,
or a record of consent for any future action.

## Object constraints

- Rhythm sharing opens only after both adults explicitly opt in.
- Every rhythm dimension is optional.
- A user may use `not_discussing_yet` for any dimension.
- Shared values are visible only within the active corridor.
- Rhythm values cannot be public, searchable, indexed, ranked, or advertised.
- The system produces conversation prompts, not match or compatibility scores.
- A difference is informational and never a rejection signal.
- Either participant may pause or revoke sharing at any time.
- Revocation and expiry clear the active shared declaration.
- The object never authorizes, predicts, records, or proves consent to a
  real-world activity.

## Always-stop conditions

- A participant reports that rhythm sharing felt like pressure to disclose,
  meet, touch, become intimate, or provide aftercare.
- A participant reports that a shared rhythm was treated as permission for a
  real-world act.
- Any rhythm value reaches discovery, ranking, recommendation, advertising,
  person-level scoring, moderation scoring, or analytics.
- Any comparison returns a numeric score, compatibility percentage, desirability
  label, warning label, or decision recommendation.
- A participant cannot revoke sharing immediately and clearly.
- Intimate content is retained beyond the active corridor or is available after
  withdrawal without a separate user-controlled copy.
- An accessibility-equivalent workflow is unavailable.
- Any money, gift-conditioned, transport-conditioned, housing-conditioned, or
  other value-linked intimacy pathway is introduced.

## Permitted measures

- Explicit participant-reported clarity, control, and comfort.
- Aggregate mutual-open, declaration, pause, revocation, and expiry counts.
- Aggregate accessibility task-completion outcomes.
- User-reported usefulness of conversation prompts.

## Prohibited measures

- Rhythm score or similarity percentage.
- Discovery-sort impact.
- Profile ranking.
- Sexual preference inference.
- Reply-speed prediction.
- Real-world outcome prediction.
- Extraction of private rhythm content into analytics, training, or advertising.
