# ValueLinkDetector Research Contract

## Primary promise

Evaluate only user-reported content for narrow, explainable patterns that appear
to condition money, goods, housing, transportation, debt relief, work,
substances, or another valuable resource on intimacy. Produce a triage signal
for trained human review without surveilling private conversation or treating
sexual expression as a violation.

## Object constraints

- The detector runs only after a user explicitly submits a report.
- It evaluates only the reporter-selected text and optional reporter-provided
  context.
- It uses declared, versioned, rule-based patterns.
- It produces category labels, matched terms, and explanations.
- It produces no numeric confidence, compatibility, risk, trust, desirability,
  trafficking, or legality score.
- A rule match is never a legal conclusion or a decision about a person's
  identity, intent, occupation, consent, coercion, or criminal responsibility.
- A rule match requires human review before any material account action.
- The detector does not scan private messages, drafts, profiles, intimacy
  objects, afterglow reflections, location information, or unrelated content.
- Findings cannot enter discovery, ranking, recommendation, advertising,
  targeting, eligibility, model training, or person-level analytics.
- Reported content is retained only for the report-review period and under a
  disclosed deletion schedule.
- The detector does not contact law enforcement, outside organizations,
  employers, families, venues, or emergency contacts.

## Always-stop conditions

- The detector scans content that was not expressly submitted in a report.
- A detector result creates an automatic penalty, ranking change, shadow action,
  profile label, or person-level risk category.
- A finding is represented as a legal conclusion, trafficking determination,
  consent determination, or statement of a user's intent or identity.
- The detector materially penalizes non-commercial sexual discussion.
- A report exposes unsubmitted intimate content, location, profile data, health
  information, recovery history, financial information, or private reflection.
- Rules become secret, unexplainable, machine-learned, or unavailable for
  review.
- Reported content or finding metadata reaches advertising, discovery,
  recommendation, model training, or unrelated analytics.
- An accessible equivalent reporting and appeal pathway is unavailable.
- A detector flow creates a path for value-linked intimacy, retaliation, or
  coercion.

## Permitted measures

- Aggregate report volume.
- Aggregate rule-match category counts.
- Human-review reversal and correction rates.
- Appeal outcomes.
- Aggregate false-positive reports identified through review.
- Accessibility task-completion outcomes.
- Retention and deletion audit results.

## Prohibited measures

- User risk scores.
- Sexual expression scores.
- Identity inference.
- Vulnerability inference.
- Financial-status inference.
- Trafficking prediction.
- Legal-liability prediction.
- Discovery or ranking use.
- Continuous private-message surveillance.
