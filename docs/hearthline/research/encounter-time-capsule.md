# EncounterTimeCapsule Research Contract

## Primary promise

Enable two adults to mutually create a short-lived, purpose-bound capsule whose
readable content is not retained by Hearthline after expiry or early deletion.

## Truthful privacy statement

Hearthline can expire and remove platform-held capsule content and key access.
Hearthline cannot guarantee that a participant did not capture, copy, manually
transcribe, photograph, record, or otherwise retain information before expiry.

## Object constraints

- Capsules are text-only in the initial prototype.
- A capsule requires a stated purpose and a mutually accepted expiry.
- The platform stores ciphertext only after mutual acceptance.
- No capsule content is included in analytics, error reporting, search,
  recommendation, discovery, advertising, model training, or moderation scores.
- The platform provides no export control, content backup, recovery workflow,
  or administrator-readable content interface.
- Either participant may delete the capsule early.
- Capsule expiry is final; renewal requires a new capsule.
- No payment identifiers, money requests, gift-conditioned arrangements,
  value-for-intimacy language, precise-location details, home/work addresses,
  live location, or media attachments are allowed.
- A capsule is never a consent record, proof of consent, or permission for a
  future real-world activity.

## Always-stop conditions

- The product claims or implies it can prevent screenshots, copying, or
  off-platform retention.
- Plaintext, encryption keys, or readable previews reach the server, logs,
  analytics, notifications, browser history, search index, cache, or backup.
- Expired or deleted capsule content can be restored through a product path.
- A capsule becomes linked to ranking, discovery, targeting, advertising,
  reputation, eligibility, moderation scoring, or person-level analytics.
- A participant cannot delete a capsule immediately.
- Any capsule permits payment, financial accounts, gifts conditioned on
  intimacy, transport-conditioned intimacy, housing-conditioned intimacy, or
  another value-linked sexual arrangement.
- An accessibility-equivalent workflow is unavailable.
- A capsule is used to pressure a person to meet, disclose, travel, become
  intimate, or continue contact.

## Permitted measures

- Aggregate acceptance, expiry, deletion, and accessibility completion counts.
- Explicit participant feedback about clarity of expiry and deletion.
- Security test results using synthetic test content only.
- Verified absence of outbound plaintext and key material in test traffic.

## Prohibited measures

- Capsule-content analysis.
- Match-quality scoring.
- Counterpart ratings.
- Read receipts.
- Screenshot detection claims.
- Real-time location collection.
- Identity, health, recovery, financial, capacity, or vulnerability inference.
