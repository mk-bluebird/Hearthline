# ArizonaComplianceCompass Research Contract

## Primary promise

Provide a static, accessible, plain-language orientation to Hearthline's Arizona
non-commercial boundary for adult connection without giving legal advice,
tracking readers, assessing fact patterns, creating acknowledgement records, or
using the content to judge users.

## Object constraints

- The Compass is read-only, non-personalized, and versioned.
- It identifies itself as general information, not legal advice.
- It links to official Arizona sources and has a scheduled legal-review date.
- It explains that Arizona prohibits sexual conduct under a fee arrangement
  involving money or other valuable consideration.
- It states that Hearthline does not support payment, rates, deposits, tips,
  payment links, gifts conditioned on intimacy, debt relief, lodging,
  transportation, labor, goods, services, or other value linked to sexual
  activity.
- It states that app activity, profile content, a match, a message, a plan, or
  a prior interaction never establishes consent to a future real-world act.
- It does not interpret individual facts, assess legal risk, or decide whether
  a user's conduct is lawful.
- It does not track views, clicks, reading time, scroll depth, copy events,
  printing, acknowledgement, or comprehension.
- It never gates an account, feature, match, message, or encounter flow.
- It has keyboard, screen-reader, text-resize, print, plain-language, and
  reduced-motion equivalents.

## Always-stop conditions

- The Compass is used to record, infer, or suggest that a user knew, accepted,
  intended, or agreed to anything.
- A view, click, read duration, scroll event, print event, or acknowledgement
  is tied to an account, device, encounter, corridor, profile, or moderation
  decision.
- The Compass is used as legal advice, fact-pattern analysis, legal-risk
  scoring, eligibility screening, or enforcement automation.
- The content becomes personalized using identity, health, recovery, sexuality,
  financial status, precise location, behavior, or inferred vulnerability.
- The page is inaccessible through an equivalent interaction modality.
- The page becomes an advertising, tracking, or engagement-optimization surface.
- The content is published without current qualified Arizona legal review.

## Permitted measures

- Version publication and withdrawal events.
- Accessibility test results.
- Static delivery availability.
- Aggregate, non-account-linked server health measurements where technically
  necessary to operate the service.

## Prohibited measures

- Reader identity.
- Read receipts.
- User acknowledgement.
- Scroll depth.
- Dwell time.
- Legal comprehension scoring.
- User segmentation.
- Personalized legal content.
- Account, discovery, corridor, or moderation linkage.
