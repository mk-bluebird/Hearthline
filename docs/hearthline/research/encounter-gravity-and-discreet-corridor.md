# EncounterGravityWell and DiscreetCorridor Research Contract

## Primary promises

EncounterGravityWell:
Enable a time-bounded, coarse-region mutual introduction without revealing
precise location, home, workplace, IP-derived location, device location,
routine, or public availability.

DiscreetCorridor:
Enable two adults to disclose only the next mutually approved layer of
conversation and planning, with immediate withdrawal and no standing consent.

## Prohibited inputs

- GPS coordinates
- IP-derived geography
- Home address
- Workplace address
- Device movement history
- Persistent location history
- Payment identifiers
- Payment links
- Financial account details
- Gift or deposit requests
- Value-for-intimacy language
- Private intimacy content in analytics or discovery

## Always-stop conditions

- A participant reports perceived location exposure.
- A participant reports unwanted contact attributable to the object.
- A participant reports pressure to meet, disclose, travel, or become intimate.
- A well or corridor event contributes to personal ranking, targeting,
  eligibility, advertising, or reputation.
- A financial, gift-conditioned, housing-conditioned, transport-conditioned,
  or other value-linked intimacy path is observed.
- A user cannot immediately withdraw or remove an active well or corridor.
- An access modality cannot complete the same core workflow.

## Permitted research measures

- Explicit user-reported comfort and control.
- Mutual introduction acceptance rate.
- User-reported clarity of disclosure states.
- Time-to-withdraw completion.
- Accessibility task completion rate.
- Aggregate expiry and revocation counts.

## Prohibited research measures

- Sexual-interest ranking
- Engagement maximization
- Profile attractiveness scoring
- Response speed scoring
- Exact-location collection
- Identity, health, recovery, financial, or vulnerability inference
- Private-intimacy content analysis
