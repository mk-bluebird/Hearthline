How Hearthline works

Hearthline is a private, low-pressure space for adults 30+ to explore friendship, conversation, activities, and community.

  • You control what you share, with whom, and for how long
  • No one is ranked, scored, or sorted by desirability
  • Every outward action requires your confirmation
  • You can block, report, or leave at any time
  • Your device, passkey type, or access needs are never shown to others
