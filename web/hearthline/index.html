Demonstration interface

A shared hearth for community conversation.

Hearthline supports human-to-human exchange. Optional AI tools may help summarize an explicitly selected discussion, translate a message, or suggest inclusive prompts—but they do not determine status, credibility, access, identity, or belonging.

Before AI assistance is enabled

AI assistance is off by default. If enabled, only text you deliberately submit for that request is eligible to be sent to a future configured service. Do not include sensitive personal, biometric, health, neural, credential, security, or institutional information in this demonstration.

Community spaces

Community principles

  • Respect self-description and communication preference.
  • Use consent before quoting, summarizing, or sharing.
  • Offer accessible, human-readable clarification.
  • Seek repair and support rather than public shaming.

AI boundaries

  • AI assistance is optional and revocable for each request.
  • AI output is a draft, never a decision or authority.
  • No automated social ranking, eligibility, or identity verification.
  • Human review and accessible appeal paths remain available.
